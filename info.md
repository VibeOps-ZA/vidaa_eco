# VIDAA Eco

Local control of Hisense **VIDAA** TVs — power (with Wake-on-LAN), volume,
inputs, apps and every practical remote key as buttons. Use it simply as a
remote, or switch on the optional **automatic power-off** for TVs left on idle
(home screen, an input with no signal, or a paused app).

- Remote, app and input buttons for dashboards, scenes and voice assistants
- Activity, Auto-off status and Network throughput sensors
- Ready-made remote-style dashboard (see the README)
- Auto power-off reads the TV's traffic from your router integration (UniFi and
  others are detected automatically)

Requires the VIDAA client certificate in `config/certs/` — see the README.
